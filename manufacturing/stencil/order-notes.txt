BLDC ESC fabrication review

Board: 116 x 112 mm, 4 layers, 1.6 mm nominal FR-4.
Copper: 2 oz finished outer layers, 1 oz inner layers.
Layer sequence: F_Cu.gtl, In1_Cu.g1, In2_Cu.g2, B_Cu.gbl.
Outer routing width and clearance: 0.200 mm. Inner logic: 0.127 mm.
The online quote forces an 8 mil global setting for 2 oz copper.
Review the actual per-layer requirements before approving this order.
Minimum via drill 0.20 mm, pad diameter 0.50 mm. Do not enlarge drills.
Four 3.20 mm non-plated mounting holes.
USB: B.Cu, In2.Cu GND reference, 90 ohm differential target.
Trace width 0.228 mm, pair gap 0.200 mm, coplanar GND gap 0.250 mm.
Confirm the stackup and USB impedance before fabrication.
No changes to routing, stack or drill sizes without approval.

Stencil: top only, 0.10 mm, frameless, manual solder-paste printing.
Use bldc-esc-F_Paste.gtp. DNP footprints are excluded in this file.
Preserve the segmented thermal-pad apertures.
Check aperture release and paste coverage before cutting.
